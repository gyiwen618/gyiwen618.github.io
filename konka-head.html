Chengdu Humanoid Robot Innovation Center · Internship · 2024

Konka-1 Head & Neck

A compact yaw and pitch assembly for a household humanoid.

Mechanical design

I designed a two-axis neck around a pair of servos for yaw and pitch. The head integrates a depth camera, a wide-angle camera, a screen, and a speaker. A retaining plate and slot hold the screen without adhesive and simplify disassembly. Ventilation openings in the rear shell and mounting plate support heat dissipation.

Fabrication

I prepared engineering drawings and coordinated with vendors to fabricate the parts.
