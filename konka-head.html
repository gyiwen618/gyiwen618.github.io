Head and Neck, Robot ‘Konka-1’

Yiwen Gao, Chengdu Humanoid Robot Innovation Center Co., Ltd.

The neck is equipped with two servos to achieve yaw and pitch movements of the head. A depth camera, wide-angle camera, and screen are mounted on the front shell of the head, while a speaker is installed on the side. The screen is fixed in place using a metal plate and a limiting groove on the head’s shell, eliminating the need for adhesive and making assembly and disassembly more convenient. To ensure optimal heat dissipation, ventilation holes are placed on the metal plate and the back shell of the head. Interestingly, the ventilation holes on the back shell form the logo of our company.
